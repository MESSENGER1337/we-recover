WE RECOVER

We Recover is a plain-spoken guide built to help you navigate the essential, practical details of rebuilding a life — from securing healthcare and finding stable housing to restarting employment and managing complex state documentation.

Instead of overwhelming you with bureaucracy or buried government forms, we organize resources by what you need to take action on this week. Clear checklists, verified contacts, and step-by-step guides mean you always know where to step next.

“When you leave a facility, everyone gives you folders full of phone numbers, but half of them lead to automated menus or wrong departments. I didn’t need another hotline. I needed one clear page that told me exactly where to go on a Tuesday morning. This site gave me that clarity.”

— Jamie R., Tulsa, Oklahoma
